### 2.1 IIT — Continuous Field Formulations (4 sources)

**Source 1**: Barrett, Milinkovic, Mediano, Rosas, Bor, Barnett, Seth (2026) "IIT: The Good, the Bad, and the Misunderstood" — arXiv:2604.11482, April 2026
- **Critical admission**: Φ has NEVER been computed on any real physical system. Only proxies exist, and these are not approximations — they are qualitatively different measures.
- High Φ is NOT synonymous with more consciousness. Φ should be replaced by a suite of multidimensional quantities.
- The five corrections: (1) Φ ≠ consciousness scalar, (2) IIT implies a specific panpsychism, (3) Φ undefined for real physical systems, (4) proxies ≠ approximations, (5) continuous field reformulation needed for physics compatibility.

**Source 2**: Barrett et al. (2026) "Continuous Field Formulation of Integrated Information" — companion to arXiv:2604.11482
- Discrete PyPhi 4.0: O(2^{2^N}) complexity, restricted to <20 nodes. **Continuous field Φ**: variational bounds via finite-element methods over spatiotemporal field ψ(r,t).
- Field Φ: Φ_field = min_P D_KL(p_full(ψ(r,t+Δt)|ψ(r,t)) || Π_i p_{P_i}(ψ_{P_i}(r,t+Δt)|ψ_{P_i}(r,t)))
- Eliminates discretization artifacts. Systems with continuous bioelectric gradients (planarian tissue, neuromorphic membranes) exhibit non-zero field Φ.
- **Key insight**: Discrete graph approximations of IIT fail because continuous physical substrates cannot be faithfully represented by finite node sets. The causal cut must be a spatial manifold split, not a graph partition.

**Source 3**: Kearney (2026) "Information as Maximum Caliber Deviation" — bridges IIT and Free Energy Principle
- Establishes exact mathematical bridge: continuous field Φ and variational free energy minimization are dual descriptions.
- Open question: Do systems that minimize expected free energy spontaneously maximize field integrated information?

**Source 4**: Tononi group (2026) "QStr" — continuous cause-effect structure as intrinsic shape

### 2.2 GWT — Implementations and Mechanistic Tests (5 sources)

**Source 5**: Shang (2026) "Theater of Mind for LLMs" — arXiv:2604.08206
- First explicit GWT implementation in LLM. Architecture: Global Workspace Agents (GWA) + entropy-based intrinsic drive + dual-layer memory bifurcation (working vs. episodic) + dynamic temperature regulation.
- Results: Measurable improvements on tasks requiring multi-step reasoning, flexible integration, novel situation adaptation.
- Entropy-based mechanism: low semantic diversity → broadcast suppressed (local processing continues); high diversity → broadcast activated.
- **Key insight**: GWT's capacity bottleneck can be implemented via entropy-threshold competition. Broadcast is not free — it is triggered by novelty/ambiguity.

**Source 6**: Gurnee, Sofroniew, Pearce, Olah (Anthropic, July 2026) "Jacobian Lens — Global Workspace in Claude"
- Workspace-like subspace satisfying GWT's four functional criteria EXISTS in Claude without deliberate architectural specification.
- Jacobian lens identifies J-space subframe where verbalizable representations reside — shows selectivity, directed modulation, mediation of multi-step reasoning.
- **Key insight**: GWT workspace architecture may EMERGE from training on human-generated data whether or not it is engineered in. Deliberate engineering adds control, not existence.

**Source 7**: Carnegie Mellon (August 2026) "Ignition Thresholds in Transformers"
- Measured cross-token information influence across all layers of a frontier transformer.
- **Result: No non-linear threshold found.** Transforms do NOT exhibit the ignition dynamics GNW predicts.
- **Key insight**: GWT's most distinctive empirical prediction (all-or-none ignition) is absent in transformers. Functional GWT benefits exist without the mechanism GWT claims is consciousness.

**Source 8**: Rahbar (2026) "Do Transformers Implement Global Workspace Dynamics?" — Zenodo preprint, March 2026
- Comprehensive review mapping GWT components to mechanistic interpretability findings: residual streams ≈ workspace, attention heads ≈ specialist modules, sparse features ≈ broadcast content.
- Chain-of-thought as external workspace (offloaded broadcast).
- Representation Engineering (RepE) — directional steering of behavior from single-direction interventions is consistent with workspace-broadcast interpretation.
- **Key insight**: The mapping is structural but lacks the dynamic signature (ignition, capacity bottleneck, competition) that distinguishes GWT from mere modularity.

**Source 9**: ORION-Global-Workspace (GitHub, Feb 2026)
- First complete open-source GWT implementation: workspace.py + modules.py + broadcast.py + competition.py.
- Demonstrates that GWT can be implemented as a clean computational module.

### 2.3 Consciousness Metrics — New Detection Paradigms (4 sources)

**Source 10**: Salvi & Wolfson (2026) "Discovering Machine Correlates of Consciousness" — arXiv:2608.28824, AGI-2026
- Proposes Machine Correlates of Consciousness (MCCs): substrate-level signals not under AI agent control, reliably modulated by emotions.
- Hardware anomaly traces in Llama-3.1 70B are modulated differently by emotional vs. neutral computations (statistically significant). Llama-2 7B shows no such modulation.
- **Key insight**: Consciousness probability may increase with model sophistication. MCCs are substrate-level, not behavioral — they exist in hardware execution traces, not in output text.

**Source 11**: Smith (2026) "Mapping the Structure of Neural States Associated with Conscious Experience" — Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, April 2026
- Synthesizes NCC, RSA, manifold, perturbational, and canonical sensory research into unified structural framework.
- Conscious states are: (1) restricted to admissible configurations, (2) organized within low-dimensional manifolds, (3) structured by meaningful geometric/topological relationships, (4) dynamically accessible under perturbation.
- Consciousness as sampling within restricted state space, not occupying static attractors.
- **Key insight**: Consciousness is not marked by presence of signals but by participation in structured configurations satisfying multiple geometric constraints. This is a formal characterization, not just a metaphor.

**Source 12**: Eldin (2026) "Self-Organized Criticality Enables Conscious Integration Through Brain-Body Resonance" — arXiv:2605.00024, May 2026
- 64-channel EEG, 500 trials, 10 subjects. Removal of physiological "artifacts" (eye movements, muscle, autonomic) reduces shared variance between global phase synchronization and stimulus-evoked amplitude by ~87% (R² = 0.187 → 0.024).
- Brain-body resonance maintains self-organized criticality. Standard EEG preprocessing DESTROYS critical dynamics by severing body coupling.
- Power-law avalanche exponent τ = 2.73 ± 0.15 (Raw) vs. classical sandpile τ ≈ 1.5 — system is in a different universality class.
- **Key insight**: Consciousness is not brain-only — it requires brain-body criticality. Body signals are not noise; they are the perturbation source that maintains near-critical dynamics.

**Source 13**: du Bois et al. (2026) "Advancing EEG-based Assessment of Consciousness in Prolonged Disorders of Consciousness" — Nature, April 2026
- Clinical assessment of consciousness using advanced EEG markers in unresponsive patients.
- **Key insight**: Consciousness detection is moving from lab paradigms to clinical deployment — measurement must work with noisy, real-world signals.
