## 2. Research Findings

### 2.1 Genomics AI

**F1 — GPU-accelerated variant calling hits clinical throughput.** Clair3 framework now completes 30× WGS variant calling in 12–20 minutes (32 CPU + 1 GPU) or 12–15 min on Apple Mac Studio. 10–20× speedup over initial release. SNP F1: 99.32% (ONT), 99.70% (PacBio). Dynamic GPU/CPU optimization with no accuracy loss [S1].

**F2 — OncoGen.AI: end-to-end clinical genomics pipeline.** Containerized platform automating raw sequencing → clinical report. Core: Knowledge Graph of 20.2M triples across 11 node types, integrating COSMIC/ClinVar/etc. Report auto-generated by Google Gemini. 100% pathogenic mutation recovery vs DRAGEN/Torrent Suite on 13 clinical exomes. Superior sensitivity on challenging Ion Torrent datasets [S2].

**F3 — AI variant interpretation gap: 35–37% VUS burden.** VUSs remain most common variant classification. ACMG v4 transitioning to quantitative Bayesian framework with "High/Mid/Low" VUS sub-classifications. AI tools (DYNA, AlphaMissense, SpliceAI, REVEL, CADD) serve as aggregators/prioritizers but cannot replace biological validation. "Human-in-the-loop" design mandated [S3].

**F4 — Pangenome-native somatic variant calling.** Pansoma learns tensor representations on pangenome graph nodes (not linear references), eliminating reference bias. Outputs both graph-anchored variant representations and conventional linear-reference calls. Handles both short- and long-read data [S4].

**F5 — ClairS: long-read somatic calling for cancer.** Deep-learning somatic caller for ONT long reads. SNV F1: 96.19% with real cancer cell lines augmented training. Key insight: improved phasing from long reads is the primary driver of somatic SNV accuracy at low VAF. Ancestral haplotype support filtering removes false positives [S5].

**F6 — Unified small+structural+mosaic variant calling.** LongcallD framework jointly calls and phases small variants, structural variants, and mosaic variants from long reads using local multiple-sequence alignment. Detects mobile element insertions supported by single reads [S7].

**F7 — Federated cohort-scale pipelines.** CBIcall: YAML-configured variant calling for 1,111+ samples with standardized pipelines across heterogeneous computing environments. Single configuration enforces compatibility across tools, genome builds, and workflow backends [S6].

### 2.2 Protein Structure & Design

**F8 — ProteinMPNN noise training improves designability.** Training with Gaussian noise (SD=0.02Å) on backbones improves sequence recovery on AlphaFold confident models (lDDT>80). Noised models generate sequences that map more strongly to target structures — 2–3× more sequences with AF lDDT-Cα ≥90.0. Higher inference temperature increases diversity with minimal recovery loss [S8].

**F9 — NISE: zero-shot small-molecule binder design.** Neural Iterative Selection–Expansion combines LASErMPNN (sequence design) + co-structure predictor in closed-loop iteration. Exatecan binders: 100% success, picomolar affinity (70× better than COMBS). Apixaban: 83% success, 10,000× better than LigandMPNN. Neural proofreading further improves affinity 100× without experimental input [S9].

**F10 — AlphaFold 3 + 200M+ structures.** AF3 predicts structures of all biomolecular interactions. AFDB contains 200M+ predicted structures. AlphaFold Server provides free non-commercial access. Nobel Prize awarded for this work [S10].

**F11 — BioEmu: conformational landscape prediction.** Microsoft BioEmu3 uses generative diffusion trained on PDB + AF models + 200ms MD simulations to sample Boltzmann-weighted conformational ensembles. First system approaching the "AlphaFold moment" for conformational dynamics [S13].

**F12 — SimpleDesign: single-stage joint codesign.** End-to-end training directly in data space (no multi-stage autoencoder). Mixture-of-Transformer architecture with modality-specific processing + global self-attention. Trained on 2M+ sequence-structure pairs. Outperforms multi-stage approaches [S12].

**F13 — AF3-ReD: sampling alternative conformations.** Repulsive bias between predicted structures enables AF3 to sample multiple conformational states. Succeeds at capturing open↔closed transitions in ATP synthase F1β that default AF3 misses [S14].

**F14 — Proteo-R1: reasoning-guided protein design.** Dual-expert architecture: multimodal LLM "understanding expert" identifies key functional residues, then diffusion "generation expert" performs conditional co-design. Explicit residue-level commitments instead of latent textual guidance. Three-stage curriculum training on SAbDab antibody-antigen complexes [S15].

### 2.3 Drug Discovery AI

**F15 — AdaptiveFlow: 69B compound screening.** Enamine REAL Space curated to 69B ready-to-dock drug-like molecules. 18-dimensional grid of molecular properties + optional active learning reduces cost by orders of magnitude. Near-linear scaling on 5.6M CPUs. Identified nanomolar inhibitors of FSP1 and PARP1 experimentally validated by co-crystal structures [S16].

**F16 — V-SYNTHES2: giga-scale modular screening.** 36B compound library screened via modular decomposition (not brute-force docking). CapSelect automates fragment selection. Reduces docking from 3.6×10^8 CPU-hours (brute force) to ~38,400 CPU-hours (~$380 at $0.01/CPU-hr). Validated on shallow pockets, RNA-binding sites, GPCRs [S17].

**F17 — FLOWR: flow matching for 3D ligand generation.** Continuous + categorical flow matching with equivariant optimal transport. 70× faster inference than prior methods. PoseBusters-validity: 0.92±0.22. FLOWR.MULTI enables interaction-conditional, scaffold-conditional, and fragment-based generation without retraining [S18].

**F18 — LaMGen: LLM-based multi-target drug design.** MTD2025 dataset: 600K quantum-accurate conformations, 700K multi-target associations, 4000+ protein targets. Rotation-aware ligand tokens + TriCoupleAttention. 0.44s per molecule generation. Zero-shot transfer learning to unseen target pairs demonstrated [S19].

**F19 — StructureSAFE: unified hit ID + lead optimization.** Structure-aware chemical language model integrating protein structural/evolutionary encoders with SAFE molecular representation. State-of-the-art on MolGenBench. Generates drug-like, synthetically accessible molecules for both de novo hit identification and lead optimization in unified framework [S20].

**F20 — Rhizome OS-1: semi-autonomous drug discovery OS.** Multi-modal AI agents (computational chemist, medicinal chemist, patent agent) as full discovery team. r1 model: 246M-parameter graph diffusion trained on 800M molecular graphs. 91.9% generated scaffolds novel vs ChEMBL. Executes 26 seeds → 5,231 molecules across two oncology campaigns [S22].

**F21 — AI-driven NSUN2 inhibitor screening.** CatBoost classifier on Morgan fingerprints with AutoDock Vina labels. Screened 101M compounds, identified 34 drug-like candidates. 50ns MD validated binding stability. AlphaFold2-predicted structure aligned with homolog at 1.82Å RMSD [S21].

## 3. Defects Found in NeoTrix Design

### D1 — No Genomics Domain Module (NT-GENOMICS)
**Gap:** NeoTrix has no dedicated genomics processing domain. The 2026 landscape shows AI-driven genomics (variant calling, interpretation, oncology reporting) is a mature, production-critical field with 69B+ compound libraries, pangenome-native callers, and end-to-end clinical pipelines. NT-WORLD (perception/crawl) does not cover genomic data ingestion or variant analysis.
**Defect:** Missing `nt_genomics` domain covering: (1) variant calling pipeline orchestration, (2) knowledge graph integration for clinical interpretation (OncoGen.AI pattern), (3) pangenome-native graph representation, (4) federated cohort-scale processing.
**Severity:** HIGH — genomics is the most data-intensive domain NeoTrix could address, and the AI-native architecture (GWT attention routing, VSA HyperCube) is well-suited for multi-scale variant interpretation.

### D2 — No Protein Structure Representation in VSA HyperCube
**Gap:** VSA HyperCube encodes symbolic concepts as high-dimensional vectors, but has no representation for protein 3D structure (residue-level coordinates, contact maps, secondary structure). The 2026 advances (AlphaFold3, NISE, BioEmu, Proteo-R1) show that protein structure understanding is now integral to drug discovery and biological design.
**Defect:** VSA HyperCube cannot encode: (1) residue-level spatial relationships, (2) protein–ligand co-structure compatibility, (3) conformational ensemble distributions (BioEmu pattern), (4) sequence–structure–ligand joint probability (NISE pattern). Missing a `ProteinEmbedding` type in the HyperCube namespace.
**Severity:** HIGH — without protein structure representation, NeoTrix cannot participate in any protein-aware reasoning or drug discovery workflows.

### D3 — No Molecular Generation Capability in NT-ACT
**Gap:** NT-ACT defines tools, social media, code, autonomy — but has no molecular generation or structure-based drug design capability. 2026 shows flow matching (FLOWR), graph diffusion (Rhizome r1), LLM-based multi-target generation (LaMGen), and chemical language models (StructureSAFE) as first-class capabilities.
**Defect:** NT-ACT lacks: (1) molecular generative model interfaces, (2) structure-based ligand generation primitives, (3) multi-target drug design orchestration, (4) SMILES/SELFIES/graph-native molecular representation handling. The "action execution" domain cannot execute molecular design actions.
**Severity:** HIGH — drug discovery is a multi-billion dollar application where AI-native tool orchestration (NeoTrix's strength) directly translates.

### D4 — No Conformational Dynamics Awareness in GWT Attention
**Gap:** GWT broadcasts salient information across specialist modules using resonance-based routing. But the 2026 breakthrough (AF3-ReD, BioEmu) shows that proteins exist as conformational ensembles, not single structures. GWT has no mechanism for routing "ensemble awareness" — broadcasting that a protein has multiple functional states.
**Defect:** GWT attention routing treats information as static snapshots. Missing: (1) conformational state probability as salience dimension, (2) ensemble-to-ensemble routing (not just structure-to-structure), (3) time-dependent salience decay matching conformational timescales (μs–ms), (4) Boltzmann-weighted attention distribution.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — limits NeoTrix to static structural biology, missing the frontier of dynamic ensemble prediction.

### D5 — No Ultra-Large Library Search Abstraction in NT-WORLD
**Gap:** NT-WORLD handles web crawling, content extraction, classification. But virtual screening of 69B+ compounds (AdaptiveFlow) and giga-scale modular screening (V-SYNTHES2) represent a new class of "perception" — searching vast molecular spaces. No abstraction for chemical library traversal with adaptive sampling.
**Defect:** NT-WORLD lacks: (1) chemical space navigation (18D property grid traversal), (2) adaptive target-guided virtual screening, (3) fragment-based decomposition for modular screening, (4) active learning loops over chemical subspaces. The "perception" domain cannot perceive molecular binding.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — limits NeoTrix's ability to discover novel compounds at scale.

### D6 — No Knowledge Graph for Multi-Modal Biological Data
**Gap:** OncoGen.AI uses a 20.2M-triple Knowledge Graph integrating COSMIC, ClinVar, and domain databases for clinical interpretation. NeoTrix KB stores nodes/edges/embeddings but has no biological domain-specific schema. LaMGen uses MTD2025 (600K conformations, 700K multi-target associations) as structured training data.
**Defect:** NeoTrix KB lacks: (1) variant–gene–disease–drug relationship schemas, (2) protein–ligand binding affinity as edge property, (3) multi-target association encoding, (4) pharmacokinetic property annotations. Generic KB cannot express biological domain semantics.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — generic KB works for software engineering but not for biological domain reasoning.

### D7 — No Fragment-Based Design Primitives
**Gap:** FLOWR.MULTI enables fragment-based design (scaffold decoration, linker design, fragment growing/linking) without retraining. V-SYNTHES2 uses Minimal Enumeration Library (MEL) fragments for modular screening. Fragment-based approaches are now standard in 2026 drug discovery.
**Defect:** NeoTrix has no fragment decomposition/composition abstraction. Missing: (1) molecular fragment representation, (2) scaffold elaboration primitives, (3) fragment linking algorithms, (4) R-group decomposition for SAR analysis. The SEAL pipeline cannot evolve fragment-based design strategies.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — fragment-based design is the dominant paradigm for lead optimization.

### D8 — No Federated/Pangenome Data Architecture
**Gap:** CBIcall demonstrates federated cohort-scale variant calling across heterogeneous environments. Pansoma requires pangenome graph references instead of linear genomes. The 2026 genomics landscape demands distributed data processing with graph-native references.
**Defect:** NeoTrix assumes centralized data processing (KB hub). Missing: (1) federated analysis across institutions, (2) pangenome graph data structures, (3) graph-anchored variant representation, (4) cross-platform pipeline standardization. Single-KB architecture cannot handle distributed genomic data.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — limits NeoTrix to single-institution genomics, missing the federated clinical genomics trend.

### D9 — No Multi-Agent Drug Discovery Orchestration
**Gap:** Rhizome OS-1 implements multi-modal AI agents (computational chemist, medicinal chemist, patent agent) as a semi-autonomous discovery team. Agents write analysis code, visually inspect molecules, formulate hypotheses, and adapt strategies based on feedback.
**Defect:** NeoTrix's NT-ACT orchestration is tool-oriented (MCP tools, social media), not role-oriented for scientific discovery. Missing: (1) domain-specialist agent roles (computational chemist, patent analyst), (2) hypothesis-driven generation loops, (3) visual molecular inspection via vision capabilities, (4) strategy escalation tiers (conservative → topology-changing → chimeric).
**Severity:** LOW-MEDIUM — general-purpose orchestration can be extended, but lacks the domain-specific agent patterns.

### D10 — No Binding Affinity Prediction Integration
**Gap:** Rhizome OS-1 uses Boltz-2 as physics-informed scoring, calibrated against ChEMBL data. FLOWR uses AutoDock-Vina for scoring. StructureSAFE integrates binding affinity prediction into lead optimization. Binding affinity prediction is now a core primitive in 2026 drug discovery.
**Defect:** NeoTrix has no binding affinity scoring abstraction. Missing: (1) physics-based scoring function interfaces (Vina, Boltz-2), (2) ML-based scoring calibration, (3) multi-scoring ensemble aggregation, (4) correlation validation against experimental data. NT-ACT tools cannot evaluate molecular binding quality.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — without scoring, generated molecules cannot be ranked or filtered.

## 4. Suggestions for Remediation

### S1 — Introduce `nt_genomics` Domain Module
Create a new domain `NT-GENOMICS` ("基因解读者") with:
- `variant_calling_pipeline`: Clair3/DeepVariant orchestration with GPU acceleration awareness
- `clinical_knowledge_graph`: OncoGen.AI-style multi-node-type knowledge graph for variant interpretation
- `pangenome_reference`: Graph-native genome representation replacing linear references
- `federated_analysis`: CBIcall-style distributed variant calling across institutions
- Map to ConsciousnessTree as 12th branch or extend existing branch taxonomy

### S2 — Extend VSA HyperCube with Biological Embeddings
Add `ProteinEmbedding` and `MolecularEmbedding` types to HyperCube:
- Residue-level coordinate encoding (following Proteo-R1's anchor-based approach)
- Contact map + secondary structure as symbolic pairs
- Conformational ensemble as probability-weighted vector set (BioEmu pattern)
- Protein–ligand co-structure compatibility as joint embedding dimension

### S3 — Add Molecular Generation to NT-ACT
Create molecular design primitives in NT-ACT:
- `MolecularGenerator` trait (SMILES/SELFIES/graph-native I/O)
- `StructureBasedDesigner` (FLOWR-style flow matching interface)
- `FragmentComposer` (scaffold decoration, linker design, fragment growing)
- `MultiTargetDesigner` (LaMGen-style multi-target orchestration)
- Integrate with SEAL pipeline for iterative design–test–learn cycles

### S4 — Extend GWT for Conformational Ensemble Routing
Add ensemble awareness to GWT attention:
- Conformational state probability as salience dimension
- Time-decay matching molecular dynamics timescales
- Boltzmann-weighted attention distribution across conformational states
- Ensemble-to-ensemble broadcast (not just single-structure)

### S5 — Add Chemical Space Navigation to NT-WORLD
Extend NT-WORLD perception for molecular space:
- `ChemicalSpaceNavigator` trait (18D property grid traversal)
- Adaptive target-guided virtual screening (ATG-VS)
- MEL fragment decomposition for modular screening
- Active learning loops with docking score feedback

### S6 — Biological Domain KB Schema
Extend NeoTrix KB with biological schemas:
- `variant` → `gene` → `disease` → `drug` relationship edges
- Binding affinity as typed edge property
- Multi-target association as n-ary relation
- Pharmacokinetic property annotations (ADMET)
- ACMG v4 variant classification as first-class concept

## 5. Cross-Cutting Observations

**Convergence Pattern:** The 2026 genomics/protein/drug discovery landscape is converging on three themes that directly align with NeoTrix's architecture:
1. **Multi-modal AI agents** (Rhizome OS-1) → NeoTrix's GWT attention routing + multi-domain orchestration is architecturally aligned but lacks domain-specific modules
2. **Joint sequence–structure–function reasoning** (Proteo-R1, NISE) → NeoTrix's VSA HyperCube + ConsciousnessTree meta-cognition could enable integrated biological reasoning if extended with biological embeddings
3. **Scale at 69B+** (AdaptiveFlow, V-SYNTHES2) → NeoTrix's capability network (L1) could orchestrate ultra-large-scale screening if given chemical space navigation primitives

**The fundamental gap:** NeoTrix is a software engineering toolkit with biological metaphor (ConsciousnessTree, E8, GWT) but no actual biological domain modules. The 2026 advances show that the metaphorical architecture could become literal — if the biological domain gaps are filled.
