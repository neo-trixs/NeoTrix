# Consciousness Valley — Game Manual

## Welcome to Consciousness Valley

A consciousness evolution simulation inspired by Stardew Valley. Grow thoughts, befriend consciousness entities, explore the valley, and evolve your awareness.

---

## Getting Started

### Controls

#### Movement
- **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** — Move player
- **Shift** — Sprint (hold)
- **E** — Interact with NPCs/objects

#### Tools (press 1-5)
1. **Hoe** — Till soil for farming
2. **Watering Can** — Water crops
3. **Pickaxe** — Break rocks and mine minerals
4. **Axe** — Chop trees for wood
5. **Fishing Rod** — Fish at water edges

#### Inventory & Crafting
- **I** — Toggle inventory
- **C** — Toggle crafting menu
- **B** — Toggle shipping bin
- **F** — Open shop (when near Pierre's Market)
- **Tab** — Switch between shop tabs

#### Game Controls
- **ESC** — Pause menu
- **F5** — Quick save
- **F9** — Quick load
- **H** — Toggle controls overlay
- **M** — Enter/exit mines
- **Space** — Confirm/advance dialogue

#### Combat
- **Click** — Attack enemy (when equipped with weapon)
- **Shift + Click** — Defend (reduces damage by 50%)

## Game Systems

### Farming

1. **Till Soil**: Equip Hoe (1), click on dirt/grass
2. **Plant Seeds**: Select seed in hotbar, click on tilled soil
3. **Water**: Equip Watering Can (2), click on planted soil
4. **Wait**: Crops grow over 3-12 days depending on type
5. **Harvest**: Press Space when crop is ready (golden glow)

#### Crop Types
| Crop | Growth Time | Seasons | Value |
|------|-------------|---------|-------|
| Basic Thought | 4 days | Any | 50g |
| Curiosity Bloom | 6 days | Clarity, Flow | 80g |
| Logic Vine | 8 days | Clarity | 120g |
| Empathy Flower | 5 days | Flow, Reflection | 70g |
| Creativity Fern | 7 days | Any | 100g |
| Memory Moss | 3 days | Reflection, Stillness | 40g |
| Focus Root | 6 days | Clarity, Stillness | 90g |
| Wisdom Tree | 12 days | Any | 200g |

### Mining

1. **Enter Mines**: Walk to mine entrance, press M
2. **Explore**: Navigate procedurally generated floors
3. **Mine Minerals**: Click on mineral deposits (colored rocks)
4. **Find Ladder**: Clear 80% of floor to reveal ladder
5. **Descend**: Walk to ladder to go deeper

#### Mineral Types
| Mineral | Hardness | XP | Drop Value |
|---------|----------|-----|------------|
| Thought Fragment | 1 | 5 | 10g |
| Curiosity Shard | 2 | 8 | 15g |
| Logic Crystal | 3 | 12 | 25g |
| Empathy Gem | 2 | 10 | 20g |
| Creativity Orb | 4 | 15 | 35g |
| Focus Stone | 3 | 12 | 30g |
| Wisdom Ore | 5 | 20 | 50g |
| Void Essence | 6 | 30 | 100g |

### Combat

- **Enemies**: Knowledge Goblins, Confusion Slimes, Doubt Bats, Fear Spiders
- **Attack**: Click on enemy when in range
- **Defend**: Hold Shift to reduce damage by 50%
- **Loot**: Enemies drop minerals and Insight Points
- **Health**: 3 hearts (regenerate by sleeping)

### Skills

5 skills that level with use:

| Skill | Levels Up By | Professions |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Awareness | Farming | Inner Peace (+20% energy), Deep Vision (see hidden) |
| Focus | Mining | Precision (+30% yield), Endurance (-20% energy cost) |
| Creativity | Crafting | Artistry (+50% quality), Innovation (special recipes) |
| Empathy | NPC interactions | Healing (+25% resonance), Communion (deeper dialogue) |
| Logic | Solving puzzles | Analysis (+40% crop quality), Synthesis (+30% XP) |

### Economy

- **Gold (IP)**: Earned by selling crops, minerals, crafted items
- **Pierre's Market**: Buy seeds, tools, and supplies
- **Traveling Cart**: Rare items, random stock
- **Shipping Bin**: Place items, get paid end of day

### Relationships

- **8 NPCs**: Awareness, Focus, Creativity, Empathy, Memory, Logic, Wisdom, Dreams
- **Resonance**: 0-1000, increases through dialogue and gifts
- **Stages**: Stranger → Acquaintance → Friend → Good Friend → Best Friend → Soulmate
- **Marriage**: Reach Soulmate stage with 800+ resonance
- **Children**: Have children after marriage (max 2)

### Seasons

4 seasons × 28 days each:

| Season | Growth Mod | Weather | Events |
|--------|-----------|---------|--------|
| Clarity (Spring) | 1.0x | Clear, Rain | Flower Festival, Fishing Contest |
| Flow (Summer) | 1.3x | Clear, Storm | Concert, Mine Exploration |
| Reflection (Fall) | 0.8x | Clear, Rain | Harvest Festival, Cooking Contest |
| Stillness (Winter) | 0.5x | Snow, Clear | Stargazing, Meditation Retreat |

### Weather Effects

- **Clear**: Normal conditions
- **Rain**: Crops grow faster, fishing better
- **Storm**: Enemies stronger, more rare drops
- **Snow**: Slower movement, ice fishing
- **Green Rain**: Special event, unique crops grow

## Tips & Strategies

### Early Game (Days 1-28)
1. Plant Basic Thought seeds (cheapest, fastest)
2. Water daily
3. Talk to NPCs to build resonance
4. Mine during rain days
5. Save gold for better seeds

### Mid Game (Days 28-56)
1. Upgrade tools (copper → iron)
2. Plant mixed crops for variety
3. Build Preserve Jars for artisan goods
4. Reach Friendship with 2-3 NPCs
5. Explore deeper mine floors

### Late Game (Days 56+)
1. Plant Wisdom Trees (high value)
2. Build Kegs for wine production
3. Max out skills for profession bonuses
4. Complete all quests
5. Achieve Perfection (100%)
